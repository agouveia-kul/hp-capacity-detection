% Draft text moved here from Data draft 1 (2026-10-02); to be extended when Section II is drafted.
The main target is the installed capacity of the HPs of an aggregate, denoted as $P_{\mathrm{TCL}}^{\max}$ as in Paper A~\cite{paperA}. For each HP household, the 99.9th percentile of its HP electricity is computed over the study period. $P_{\mathrm{TCL}}^{\max}$ is the sum of these percentiles over the HP households of the aggregate. The percentile is used instead of the maximum to reduce the influence of single spurious readings.
