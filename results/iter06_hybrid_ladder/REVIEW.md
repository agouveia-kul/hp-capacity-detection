**Problems found:**
- **The learned scale carries no information within a pool.** L4b's α lands at 10³–10⁵ (GB 9/10 seeds, B\* 8/10, replication 10/10); m̂_h is flat at ≈ 0.025 against a true m_h spread of 0.01–0.045 (`m_hat_vs_true.png`). Zero-residual `paperA_l4a` / `l4b` vs `paperA_corr`: +1.2 / +0.3 pp GB, +0.8 / −0.5 pp CH. Within a pool L4 − L3 therefore measures the corrected vs the uncorrected base, and the corrected base is invalid (ŝ ≤ 0) in 8 % (max 11 %) of GB ≤ 15 % test substations (CH 1 %, max 4 %).
- **The R(i) update row breaks at ×0.5:** median WAPE 2,041 % (TabPFN), 1,287 % (CNN). Not a code error: the re-estimated base is right (y / base ≈ 1.0 at every p), but the frozen residual multiplies it by exp(f) ≈ 25 at p = 0.05 and 4 at p = 0.1, because at ×1.0 it learned to inflate a near-zero corrected base from features that the shift moves. The residual is not separable from the base it was trained on. At ×1.5 the update row is sane (+7.0 / +13.2 / +12.0 pp; L0 update +8.1).
- **S-GB n = 32: the usable designs differ by job type** (residual jobs and the linear job 7 of 20; CNN direct / L4 11; TabPFN direct / L4 15; inner CV infeasible in the rest, the 2026-11-02 L4b fix only stopped the crash). n = 32 and n\* therefore compare families on different designs; n = 62 (the primary S point) has 20/20 everywhere. Cause of the per-job difference not yet traced.
- **Compute:** 2026-10-26 19:11 → 2026-11-09 23:50 (14.2 days wall, 775 job-h in completed jobs, 96 failed attempts: timeouts, 9 OOM, 2 IT-managed restarts) against an estimate of ~300 job-h / 50 h. GB CNN seeds 2 and 9 train to ~200 epochs × 3 initialisations (up to 35 ks per job). Below-normal priority was not the cause. The async timeout fires up to 2.5 h late.
- B1 initialisation spread (diagnostic): 63–67 % of final fits keep a non-first initialisation; test-WAPE spread median 2.2 pp (GB) / 2.7 (CH), max 385 pp (one collapsed initialisation, screened out).
- Reload check of the 240 persisted rung winners (`models/{a_gb,a_ch}/reload_check.csv`): all 240 load and reproduce their test predictions (max relative difference 1.6 × 10⁻⁵, |ΔWAPE| ≤ 0.0002 pp, no load errors), so 07 can use them as they are.

**Decisions for Alex:**
1. **The hybrid claim (rule 2) fails, as pre-registered; what does the paper's method become?** What pays is identifiability-guided *inputs* and *target* (L2 for linear and TabPFN, L3 for linear; CNN indifferent), not the learned scale, which collapses to the pilot constant. Options: (i) report the failure and make "physics features + physics target" (L2/L3) the method, labelled post hoc; (ii) pre-register a revised L4 in a new iteration before 07 (e.g. fall back to L3 where r̂ or ŝ marks the base as unidentified, ≤ 15 %); (iii) keep L4 as is and let 07 show transfer only. *Recommended:* (i) for the paper, plus (ii) only if 07 shows that m̂_h transfers across pools (the one place a learned scale could matter, since T_design differs).
2. **L5 and the R update mode.** *Recommended:* drop L5 (rule 3; report it as a negative result) and do not offer the update row as a deployment mode for L4 (it fails at ×0.5); keep it as a documented failure in the robustness section.
3. **What 07 carries.** L4a and L4b split the inner-CV wins (GB 19 / 11 of 30 family × seed, CH 10 / 20) with test medians within 0.4 pp. *Recommended:* 07 runs every rung as planned with L4a as the L4 default (one pilot parameter κ, no fitted regression); before 07, trace and fix the n = 32 design difference (a small code item) and add the CNN epoch cap / per-job timeout to the 07 runtime estimate.
